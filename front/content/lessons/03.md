---
title: Ko Kai
---

Thai has many letters that carry the same sound.  
But there's only one letter for the `/k/` sound - as a result, it's one of the most frequent.  
Thai language doesn't have a separate `/g/` phoneme and a sepatate `g` letter - 
so foreign words with `g` will most commonly use ก to transcribe it.

<br>

• ก /k/, used to transcribe `g` in foreign words and names

<br>

##### words:  

[กา](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/กา) `/kaː/` - crow, teapot   
[นาก](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/นาก) `/naːk/` - otter  
[ราก](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ราก) `/raːk/` - root  
[กาก](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/กาก) `/kaːk/` - leftover

title: First Letters
---

Thai alphabet has a lot of letters.  
But we don't need to know them all yet.  
We'll start with the most common ones.  
For now, I won't be specifying the tone in transcription - we'll learn about it later.

Most Frequent Thai Consonants:

• น `/n/`  
• ร `/r/`

Most Frequent Thai Vowels:

• า `/aː/` ː this symbol means long vowel

syllables:  

นา `/naː/` - rice field  
รา `/raː/`  - mould  
นาน `/naːn/` - long

---
title: Vowel carrier
---

Thai alphabet is an [abugida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abugida),
it means vowels are attached to consonants and cannot stand independently.
So, if we want to write just /aː/ without a consonant, we need to use the so-called vowel carrier.

• อ /oː/ or vowel carrier  


with vowel carrier:

[อา](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/อา) `/aː/`   
[อาน](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/อาน) `/aːn/` - saddle

with อ as a vowel `/oː/` :

[กอ](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/กอ) `/koː/` - _classifier_   
[กอน](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/กอน) `/koːn/` - child   
[กอก](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/กอก) `/koːk/` - olive

[นอ](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/นอ) `/noː/` - horn   
[นอน](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/นอน) `/noːn/` - to sleep   
[นอก](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/นอก) `/noːk/` - outside

[รอ](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/รอ) `/roː/` - to wait   
[รอน](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/รอน) `/roːn/` - to reduce     
[รอก](http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/รอก) `/roːk/` - pulley

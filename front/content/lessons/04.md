---
title: Vowel carrier
---

Thai alphabet is an [abugida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abugida),
it means vowels are attached to consonants and cannot stand independently.  
So, if we want to write just /aː/ without a consonant, we need to use the so-called vowel carrier.

<br>

• อ /ɔː/ or vowel carrier <PlayPhoneme p="ɔ"></PlayPhoneme>   

<br>

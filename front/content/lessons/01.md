This course is slow-paced, with each lesson designed to be around 5 minutes.  

Pronunciation will be written in either 
[IPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Phonetic_Alphabet) inside slashes like this `/ta/`

or [Paiboon](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Thai_romanization)
<br>

Exercises will also accept IPA or Paiboon

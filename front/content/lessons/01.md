This course is slow-paced, with each lesson designed to be around 5 minutes.  

Pronunciation will be written in either
<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Phonetic_Alphabet" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">IPA</a>
inside slashes like this `/ta/`

or <a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Thai_romanization" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Paiboon</a>

<br>

Exercises will also accept IPA or Paiboon

title: Tone rules
---


Thai has 5 tones.  
They are shown with a combination of:  
- consonant class
- syllable type
- vowel length
- diacritic marks

<br>

##### diacritic marks:

- ่ 1
- ้ 2
- ๊ 3
- ๋ 4

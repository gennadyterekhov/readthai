---
title: Vowel carrier อ part 2
---

The vowel carrier also has another meaning when used after a consonant - it makes the /ɔː/ sound.  
So , when อ is at the start of a word or with vowel markings, it acts as a carrier,
but when it's after another consonant - it's just /ɔː/

title: Vowel E
---

Thai has several letters that stand before the consonant, the most common of them are:

• เ /eː/ upper-e , pronounced in the upper part of the mouth <PlayPhoneme p="e"></PlayPhoneme>  
• แ /ɛː/ lower-e , pronounced in the lower part of the mouth <PlayPhoneme p="ɛ"></PlayPhoneme>
